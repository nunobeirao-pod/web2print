UX Architecture & Experience Foundation

The structural blueprint for a direct-to-consumer textile customisation platform — apparel personalised with DTF and embroidery, designed for consumers and creators. Everything downstream (design system, screens, flows) builds on this document.

Audience · Consumers & creators

Model · Direct storefront — we print & ship
01 — Experience principles

Four influences, one synthesis

Simplicity of Apple
One clear action per screen

Progressive disclosure everywhere. Defaults are excellent; options appear only when asked for. No screen has two competing calls to action.

Editor of Canva
Direct manipulation, zero fear

Everything on the garment is draggable, resizable, undoable. Autosave is constant. The user never loses work and never sees a modal that blocks the canvas.

Commerce of Shopify
Price is always live and honest

Every configuration change updates the price instantly. Checkout is fast, trusted, and never surprises. Order status is transparent from press to doorstep.

Discovery of Printful
The product is the hero

Catalogue browsing is visual-first: real garment photography, colour/size availability at a glance, print-method guidance built into discovery.

What makes it unique: the customiser is the storefront. Every product image site-wide is an entry point into editing — "Customise" is never more than one tap away, and AI can draft the first design from a sentence so no one starts from a blank garment.

03 — Navigation model

Three navigation contexts

Storefront chrome
Header: logo · Shop (mega-menu by garment) · Create (AI entry) · search · cart · account
Persistent: "Customise" pill on every product surface
Footer: help, print guides, shipping, legal
Customizer chrome
Top bar: back · design name (autosave state) · undo/redo · live price · Add to cart
Left rail: tools — Text, Upload, AI, Clipart, Layers
Right panel: contextual properties of selection
Bottom: garment view switcher (front/back/sleeve), colour & size
Dashboard chrome
Sidebar: Overview · Orders · My Designs · Uploads library · Addresses · Settings
Creators see extra: Published designs · Earnings
Every design card: Edit → reopens customizer; Reorder → straight to checkout

08 — Responsive behaviour

One system, three breakpoints

Mobile
< 768px
· Bottom tab bar: Shop / Create / Cart / Account
· Customizer: full-screen canvas, tools as a bottom sheet; properties slide over the sheet, never over the garment
· Catalogue: 2-col grid, filters as a full sheet
· Hit targets ≥ 44px throughout
Tablet
768 – 1119px
· Header nav, no tab bar
· Customizer: left rail collapses to icons; right panel overlays on selection
· Catalogue: 3-col grid, filter rail collapsible
· Dashboard sidebar becomes top segmented nav
Desktop
≥ 1120px
· Full three-zone customizer (rail + canvas + panel)
· Catalogue: 4-col grid, persistent filter rail
· Mega-menu on Shop hover
· Max content width 1440px, centred
