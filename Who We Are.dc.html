Who we are

We turn ideas into something you can wear.

From a photo, a memory, a sentence or simply an idea in your head — we make it easier to turn something personal into something real.

Most ideas never leave the screen. We wanted to change that.

You might have a photo you love.
An inside joke only your friends understand.
A memory you want to keep.
Or an idea that exists only in your head.

Turning that into something you can actually wear shouldn't require design skills, complicated software or a conversation with a printing company.

That's why we created a simpler way to go from idea to piece.

The Studio

Technology when it helps. Humans where it matters.

We use technology to make creating easier — not to take the creative decision away from you.

Our tools can help you start, improve an image or explore an idea. But you're always in control of what gets made.

And when your design leaves the screen, real people take over.

Production. Quality control. Packing. Support.

Digital where it makes things easier. Human where it makes things better.

What matters to us.

No surprises.

We'd rather tell you what to expect than promise something we can't deliver. From the design to the final piece, clarity matters.

Good enough isn't enough.

The garment matters as much as what's printed on it. A great idea deserves a piece you'd actually want to wear.

Easy doesn't mean impersonal.

Technology should remove friction, not human connection. When you need help, there should still be a person on the other side.

Always getting better.

We test, learn and improve. From the Studio to the products and the way we produce them, there's always a better version to build.

Some things are too personal to come off a shelf.

A birthday. • A friendship. • A stupid joke.
A photograph. • A team. • A memory.
An idea that only makes sense to you.

That's exactly the point.
