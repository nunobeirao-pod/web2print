Tablet adaptation: Hero keeps Desktop's 2-col split (text+image), already fluid. Volume pricing keeps the intentional horizontal scroll (5 tiers don't fit comfortably as fixed columns at 768px). Features go from Desktop's 4-up to 2-up (Mobile was 1-up) — an intermediate composition. The quote form's wide multi-column row (image/product/area/qty/price/subtotal) doesn't fit at 768px without cramming, so it uses the same compact stacked-card composition as Mobile, just at the wider Tablet container — this is the one place Tablet leans on the Mobile pattern, because the Desktop row genuinely doesn't fit.
